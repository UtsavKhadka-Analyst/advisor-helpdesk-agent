# Juniper Ridge Financial: Firm Profile

> **Fictional firm.** Juniper Ridge Financial, its staff and its clients are invented
> for this project. All data in this repository is synthetic.

## Overview

Juniper Ridge Financial is a financial planning firm with 40 staff, working from one
office in a mid-sized US town. It serves about 900 client households, most of them
approaching or in retirement.

## Services

- Retirement planning
- Insurance reviews
- Tax-season coordination: collecting client documents and working with each
  client's tax preparer

The firm does not prepare tax returns or give legal advice.

## Staff

| Role | Headcount | Main responsibilities |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Advisor | 12 | Client relationships and recommendations |
| Paraplanner | 6 | Plan preparation and research |
| Client Service | 10 | Client requests, paperwork and correspondence |
| Operations | 4 | Processes, systems and account transfers |
| Compliance | 3 | Regulatory questions, complaints and record keeping |
| Management and Administration | 5 | Leadership, supervision and office administration |
| **Total** | **40** | |

## Tools

Staff work in Outlook, Teams, SharePoint and Excel.

## Why a help desk

Staff spend time searching policy documents and writing routine client replies.
The industry is regulated, so anything sent to a client must be reviewed by a person.

All staff can use the help desk. The main users are Client Service and Paraplanners.
It answers policy questions with the source document, drafts client replies,
escalates what it cannot answer, and drafts knowledge-base articles for repeated
questions.

## Review and escalation

| Situation | Handled by |
| --- | --- |
| Approval of a drafted client reply | Client service manager |
| Approval when the manager is unavailable or wrote the request | Compliance officer |
| Complaints and regulatory questions | Compliance officer |
| Process and technical questions | Operations lead |

No one approves their own request. The client service manager is part of the
Management and Administration group.

## Limits

- The help desk gives no investment, tax or legal advice.
- It never sends anything to a client without a person's approval.
- It works only from the firm's policy documents.

## Demonstration setup

The project is built and tested by one person, who plays both the staff and reviewer
roles. Test emails go only to the builder's own address. The tools shown in the demo
depend on what the trial account includes.
