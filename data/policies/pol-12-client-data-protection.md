# POL-12: Client Data Protection

> Synthetic policy for the fictional firm Juniper Ridge Financial. It is not regulatory
> guidance.

| Owner | Last reviewed | Next review |

| Compliance officer | November 1, 2026 | November 1, 2027 |

## Purpose

To limit how client personal information is used and exposed.

## Rules

1. Access only the client information needed for the task.
2. Do not enter client names, account numbers, identification numbers or other personal
   details into the help desk or any other AI tool.
3. Describe questions in general terms, such as "a client has moved". Drafts use
   placeholders such as "[Client name]", filled in outside the AI tool.
4. Do not copy client information into knowledge-base articles or logs.
5. Store client information only in approved firm systems.
6. Lock your screen when away from your desk.

## Reporting a problem

Report suspected loss, misdirection or unauthorised access to the Compliance officer
the same business day. Do not try to investigate it alone.
