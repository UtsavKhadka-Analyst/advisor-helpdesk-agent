# POL-03: Address and Contact Detail Changes

> Synthetic policy for the fictional firm Juniper Ridge Financial. It is not regulatory
> guidance.

| Owner | Last reviewed | Next review |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Client service manager | November 1, 2026 | November 1, 2027 |

## Purpose

To keep client records accurate and prevent unauthorised changes.

## Steps for an address change

1. Obtain a signed address change form through the secure portal, in person or by post.
2. Verify identity under POL-02.
3. Update the client record within 2 business days of completing steps 1 and 2.
4. Send a confirmation to both the old and the new address.
5. Tell the client's advisor.

## Phone number and email changes

Follow the same steps. Make the verification call to the old phone number. If the old
number no longer works, the client must verify in person with photo ID.

## Not accepted

Changes requested only by email or by a phone call.

## Account providers

Where an account provider holds its own address record, send the provider's form to
the client and track it until the provider confirms the change.
