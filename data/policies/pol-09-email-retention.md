# POL-09: Email Retention

> Synthetic policy for the fictional firm Juniper Ridge Financial. The retention period
> is invented and is not regulatory guidance.

| Owner | Last reviewed | Next review |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Compliance officer | November 1, 2026 | November 1, 2027 |

## Purpose

To keep a complete record of business email.

## Rules

1. Keep all business email for 7 years from the date sent or received.
2. File client email to the client record within 2 business days.
3. Do not delete business email before the retention period ends.
4. Do not use personal email accounts for firm business.
5. Report business sent from a personal account to Compliance and forward a copy to the
   firm's system.

## Legal hold

When Compliance places a hold, keep the affected records until the hold is released,
even if the 7 years have passed.

## Deletion

Compliance approves deletion of records after the retention period.
