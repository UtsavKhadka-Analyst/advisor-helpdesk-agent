# POL-11: Tax-Season Document Collection

> Synthetic policy for the fictional firm Juniper Ridge Financial. Dates are invented
> and are not tax filing deadlines.

| Owner | Last reviewed | Next review |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Operations lead | November 1, 2026 | November 1, 2027 |

## Purpose

To collect client documents and pass them to each client's tax preparer.

## Schedule

| Date | Action |
| --- | --- |
| January 15 | Send the document checklist to participating clients |
| February 28 | Date by which clients are asked to upload documents |
| First week of March | Follow up on missing documents |

If a date falls on a weekend, use the next business day.

## Rules

1. Collect documents through the secure portal.
2. Share them with a tax preparer only after the client has signed a consent form.
3. Send them to the preparer within 3 business days of receiving both the documents and
   the consent.
4. Follow POL-01 for all sharing.

## Limits

The firm does not prepare tax returns or give tax filing advice. Direct tax questions
to the client's tax preparer.
