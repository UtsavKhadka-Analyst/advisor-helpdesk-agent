# POL-02: Client Identity Verification

> guidance.

## Purpose

To confirm that a request comes from the client or an authorised person.

## When verification is required

Before changing an address, contact details or a beneficiary, transferring an account,
granting portal access or sharing account information. New clients are verified with
photo ID during onboarding under POL-06.

## Standard verification

1. Call the client on the phone number already on file. Do not use a number supplied
   in the request.
2. Confirm two further details held on the client record.
3. Record the date, the method and the staff member in the client record.

An email alone never verifies identity. Verification applies only to the request for
which it was done.

## In person

A current government-issued photo ID may replace the call.

## If verification fails

Do not act on the request. Tell the Compliance officer the same business day.
