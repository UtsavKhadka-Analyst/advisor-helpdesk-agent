# POL-05: Account Transfers

> Synthetic policy for the fictional firm Juniper Ridge Financial. It is not regulatory
> guidance.

| Owner | Last reviewed | Next review |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Operations lead | November 1, 2026 | November 1, 2027 |

## Purpose

To move client accounts between providers safely.

## Steps

1. Obtain a signed transfer authorisation and a recent account statement through the
   secure portal.
2. Verify identity under POL-02.
3. The advisor confirms that the transfer has been discussed with the client.
4. Operations submits the request within 3 business days of receiving complete
   paperwork.
5. Operations checks progress and the client receives a status update every week until
   the transfer is complete.

## Timeframes

A transfer usually takes 5 to 10 business days after submission. Providers control the
timing, so staff must not promise a completion date.

## Rejected transfers

Operations tells the client and the advisor within 1 business day of a rejection and
explains what is needed to resubmit.
