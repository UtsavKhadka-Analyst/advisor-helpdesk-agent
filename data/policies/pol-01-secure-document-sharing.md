# POL-01: Secure Document Sharing

> Synthetic policy for the fictional firm Juniper Ridge Financial. It is not regulatory
> guidance.

| Operations lead | November 1, 2026 | November 1, 2027 |

## Purpose

To protect client documents when they are sent or received.

## Rules

1. Share client documents through the firm's secure client portal.
2. Do not attach documents containing personal or account information to email.
3. Do not use personal email, personal cloud storage or messaging apps.
4. Portal sharing links expire after 7 days. Send a new link if one has expired.
5. Before sharing with anyone other than the client, obtain the client's signed consent.
6. Verify identity under POL-02 before giving a person new portal access.

## If a client sends a document by email

1. Save it to the client record.
2. Reply without the attachment and direct the client to the portal for future files.
3. Keep the email under POL-09.

## Exceptions

If a client cannot use the portal, they may deliver documents in person or by post.
Report any document sent to the wrong person to Compliance under POL-12.
