# POL-07: Client Communications and Review

> Synthetic policy for the fictional firm Juniper Ridge Financial. It is not regulatory
> guidance.

| Owner | Last reviewed | Next review |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Compliance officer | November 1, 2026 | November 1, 2027 |

## Purpose

To make sure client communications are accurate and reviewed.

## Response time

Acknowledge a client message within 1 business day. An acknowledgement that only
confirms receipt and the expected response date, using the approved template, does not
need approval. Complaints have their own timeframes under POL-08.

## Review before sending

1. All other drafted client replies need approval before they are sent, including every
   reply drafted by the help desk.
2. The client service manager approves. The Compliance officer approves when the
   manager is unavailable or wrote the draft or request.
3. No one approves their own draft or request.
4. A rejected draft returns to the writer with comments.
5. Complaint replies follow POL-08 instead.

## Content

- Do not promise investment results or completion dates controlled by others.
- Only advisors give investment recommendations.
- The firm does not give legal advice or prepare tax returns.
- Do not include full account numbers or government identification numbers.
