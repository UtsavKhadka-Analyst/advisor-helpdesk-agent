# POL-04: Beneficiary Changes

> Synthetic policy for the fictional firm Juniper Ridge Financial. It is not regulatory
> guidance.

## Purpose

To record a client's beneficiary choices correctly.

## Steps

1. Send the account provider's or insurer's beneficiary form through the secure portal.
2. The account owner completes and signs it. Some providers also require a witness or
   a spouse's signature, as stated on their form.
3. Verify identity under POL-02.
4. The advisor reviews the form for completeness.
5. Submit it to the provider within 3 business days of receiving a complete form.
6. Provider confirmation usually takes 10 business days. File it in the client record
   and tell the client.

## Not accepted

Changes requested by email or phone, and forms signed by someone other than the account
owner unless Compliance has accepted proof of legal authority.

## Advice

Staff do not tell clients whom to name. Legal and estate questions go to the client's
own attorney.
