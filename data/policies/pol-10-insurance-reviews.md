# POL-10: Insurance Reviews

> Synthetic policy for the fictional firm Juniper Ridge Financial. It is not regulatory
> guidance.

| Owner | Last reviewed | Next review |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lead advisor | November 1, 2026 | November 1, 2027 |

## Purpose

To check that a client's insurance still fits their circumstances.

## When

- Once a year for each client who receives this service
- After a major life event, such as marriage, divorce, a birth, a death or retirement

## Steps

1. Client Service requests current policy statements through the secure portal at least
   10 business days before the meeting.
2. A paraplanner prepares a summary of current cover.
3. The advisor meets the client and discusses any gaps.
4. The written meeting summary is sent within 5 business days, after approval under
   POL-07.

## Restrictions

Only a licensed advisor recommends insurance products. A client should not be told to
cancel existing cover before any replacement is in force.
