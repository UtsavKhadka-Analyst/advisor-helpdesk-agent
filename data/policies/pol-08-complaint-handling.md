# POL-08: Complaint Handling

> guidance.

## Purpose

To handle complaints promptly and fairly.

## What counts as a complaint

Any expression of dissatisfaction about the firm's service, advice, fees or staff,
whether spoken or written. If unsure, treat it as a complaint.

## Steps

1. Pass the complaint to the Compliance officer and record it the same business day.
2. Compliance acknowledges it to the client within 2 business days of receipt.
3. Compliance investigates and sends a written response within 15 business days of
   receipt. If more time is needed, Compliance tells the client why and gives a new date.
4. Record the outcome and actions in the complaint log.

## Who may reply

Only Compliance replies to a complaint. A second Compliance officer reviews each
written response before it is sent. Other staff must not draft or send a reply,
apologise on the firm's behalf for fault, or offer compensation.
