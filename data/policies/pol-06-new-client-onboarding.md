# POL-06: New-Client Onboarding

> Synthetic policy for the fictional firm Juniper Ridge Financial. It is not regulatory
> guidance.

| Owner | Last reviewed | Next review |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Client service manager | November 1, 2026 | November 1, 2027 |

## Purpose

To set up new clients completely and consistently.

## Steps

1. Send a welcome message within 1 business day of the signed client agreement. It
   follows POL-07.
2. Check a current government-issued photo ID.
3. Create the client record and portal access.
4. Collect the client questionnaire and account statements through the portal.
5. Compliance reviews the file.
6. The advisor holds the first planning meeting.

## Timeframe

Onboarding is normally completed within 15 business days of the signed agreement. If
documents are missing after 10 business days, Client Service follows up and tells the
advisor.

## Restrictions

No account transfer is submitted until the Compliance review is complete.
